## Summary

**Legendary Forge** is the build-it diorama venture: a plug-and-play bookshelf
diorama platform — a magnetic grid, snap-in lighting and motion modules, and
Bluetooth audio — that a parent and child build together in one afternoon with no
coding or soldering. The business model is razor-and-blades (an accessible Starter
Kit plus high-margin Expansion Packs). It is sold through
[legendary-arena.com/diorama](https://www.legendary-arena.com/diorama).

## Why it's not on this wiki

The diorama is a **separate venture** from the Legendary Arena engine. Its build IP
— the bill of materials, the magnetic-grid contact engineering, the connector
design — is a sellable asset, so it is kept out of this (public) engine wiki and
out of the engine monorepo's git history. If the venture is ever sold, the buyer
takes the `legendary-forge` repo whole; this pointer page is simply removed.
