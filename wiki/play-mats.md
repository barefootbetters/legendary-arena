# Play Mats

## Summary

A **play mat** is the physical surface a Marvel Legendary game is set up on —
either a printed board with labelled card zones (City, HQ, Mastermind, Scheme,
etc.) or plain art you lay the cards on yourself. This page catalogues the mats
that exist for the tabletop game so the team building the digital
[Play Board](play-board.md) and any future merchandise
([Legendary Forge](legendary-forge.md)) has one reference for what the printed
layouts look like and where they come from. It is a **product/reference page**,
not an engine spec — the digital board is authoritative for zone behaviour, not
any one physical mat.

Marvel Legendary mats fall into two groups: **official Upper Deck mats** (some
with game-specific card spaces, some just art) and **fan-made printable
layouts** that cover the later expansions the official mats never caught up to.

## Mechanics

### The zone layout every layout mat shares

Every labelled Legendary play mat prints the same core set of zones, which is
exactly the zone vocabulary the engine projects to the
[Play Board](play-board.md):

| Mat label | Engine zone |
|---|---|
| TWISTS | Scheme-twist stack (Scheme deck) |
| SCHEME | Active [Scheme](scheme.md) |
| ESCAPE | Escaped villains pile |
| WOUNDS | Wound stack |
| BYSTANDERS | Rescued bystanders |
| STRIKES | Master Strike discard |
| MASTERMIND | The Mastermind + its tactics (see [Master Strike](master-strike.md)) |
| CITY (BRIDGE · STREETS · ROOFTOPS · BANK · SEWERS) | The five City spaces |
| VILLAIN DECK | The villain deck |
| SIDEKICKS · OFFICERS | Sidekick / Officer piles |
| HERO DECK | The HQ recruitment deck |
| HQ | The five HQ recruit slots |

This shared vocabulary is why a single digital board renders every scenario: the
mat art changes per theme, the zones do not.

### Official playmats

The original core set shipped with a cardboard game board. Upper Deck later sold
a rubber play mat version of that layout (2014), plus themed mats.

- **Original-style layout mat** (City, HQ, Mastermind, Scheme, Escaped Villains,
  Wounds, Bystanders, decks) — the Loki vs. The Avengers art shown above. The
  2014 rubber accessory is long out of print; used copies surface on BGG
  GeekMarket and eBay.

**Themed Legendary playmats** (about 32.5″ × 14.5″, rubber-backed, with extra
card spaces versus the old cardboard board):

- **Dark Phoenix vs. The X-Men** — listed at $29.99 on the Upper Deck store. Art
  by Gil Martimiano da Silva Jr.
- **Thanos vs. The Avengers** — art by Caio Cacau (the mat shown above). Often
  sold out; Amazon has listed it as unavailable. Reviews say it works as a board
  replacement with extra card spots.
- The **Villains** expansion also included its own play mat.

**Generic Marvel art mats** (24″ × 13.5″, usable with any game, no Legendary
City/HQ labels): Thanos, Spider-Man, and Wolverine designs, around $20 on
Amazon / the Upper Deck store.

**Newest official option:** *Legendary: A Marvel Deck Building Game (Second
Edition)* (2026) includes a rubber-backed deluxe play mat in the $99.99 core
box, alongside 550 cards, a rulebook, and a keyword guide. It is compatible with
older expansions.

**Organized Play.** Upper Deck distributed the Loki vs. The Avengers mat through
the *Legendary Organized Play Kit #1* (2017), bundled with an acetate Loki
Mastermind card, foil Thor rares, and 25 alternate-art foil "Unleash the Power
of the Cosmic Cube" scheme cards:

![Legendary Organized Play Kit #1 promotional sheet — the Loki vs. The Avengers play mat surrounded by the kit contents: an acetate Loki Mastermind card, a foil Thor rare, and an Unleash the Power of the Cosmic Cube scheme card, with an "Order Now — coming February 2017" burst.](/play-mats/organized-play-kit-1.jpg "width=55%")

### Fan-made / custom print mats

These are what most long-time players use once they own many expansions
(Sidekicks, Horrors, Infinity Shards, extra City slots, and so on), because the
official themed mats stopped adding spaces years before the expansions did.

Best starting point is the **BoardGameGeek Files** section for Legendary. Popular
free designs:

- **zeroeo (updated 2020)** — full table mats (~24.5″ × 28.5″) plus a separate
  Mastermind mat, with extra spaces for expansions. Built for Inked Gaming
  two-player / oversized printing.
- **MrWorley Universal Mat** — a 14″ × 28″ oversized layout, updated for
  Sidekicks / Horrors.
- Many player-specific and themed variants (Avengers, X-Men, Spider-Man,
  minimalist).

**GitHub collection — [ruler501/Marvel](https://github.com/ruler501/Marvel).** A
print-and-play repo with `Playmats/` and `Examples/` folders plus box-cutout
templates and card-logo art, sized for 10″ printing — box inserts and mat files
in one place.

**Where people print them:**

- **Inked Gaming** — custom oversized (~14″ × 28″) or two-player (~24″ × 28″)
  mats; a common recommendation in the Legendary community. Best value during
  sales.
- **Etsy** — digital JPG downloads (e.g. 14″ × 28″ X-Men / Avengers layouts) you
  send to a printer yourself.

## Interactions

- **[Play Board](play-board.md)** — the digital analog. The engine projects the
  same zone set the physical mats label (City / HQ / Mastermind / Scheme /
  villain deck / Wounds / Bystanders / decks) into `UIState`, which the
  arena-client renders. The mat art is decorative; the zone semantics live in
  the engine, not on any mat.
- **[Legendary Forge](legendary-forge.md)** — the physical-product line. If
  Legendary Arena ever ships its own branded mat, it sits in this catalogue and
  reuses the zone layout above.
- **[IP Licensing](ip-licensing.md)** — every official mat here is a Marvel /
  Upper Deck product. Reproducing or selling mat art is a licensing question,
  not just a design one; fan print files live in a grey area their authors
  distribute for personal use.
- **[Card Image Acquisition](card-image-acquisition.md)** — the same
  `legendarycardgame.com` scrape that sources card art also happens to host
  these mat scans; they were pulled with the same staging tooling.

## Edge Cases

- **Official themed mats don't have every expansion slot.** They print the
  base-game zones plus a few extras; if you own a large collection (Sidekicks,
  Bystander variants, extra City space), the BGG fan files are usually more
  complete.
- **The "generic art" mats have no labels.** The 24″ × 13.5″ Thanos /
  Spider-Man / Wolverine mats are plain art — they don't teach or enforce any
  zone layout, so don't mistake one for a layout mat.
- **The Thanos image on this page is a labelled *layout* mat, not the generic
  art mat.** Same character, different product — the one shown has the full
  City/HQ zone print (and Caio Cacau's signature).
- **Availability drifts.** The 2014 original mat and the Thanos mat are
  frequently out of print or sold out; prices quoted here were current at
  `last-reviewed` and will move.
- **The digital board is not bound to any physical mat's layout.** Nothing in
  the engine depends on a mat existing or matching pixel-for-pixel; the mats are
  a design reference, not a contract.
