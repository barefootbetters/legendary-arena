# WP-879 — Schemes in the Effect-Implementation Index (Registry + Tooling + Dashboard)

**Layer:** Registry (schema) + Shared Tooling (index generator) + App
(dashboard `/debug/effects`) · **Lane:** Standard two-session (cross-layer;
registry contract surface) · **Baseline:** `origin/main` @ `4fd75a15`
(re-confirm at execution) · **User-Visible Surface:**
dashboard.legendary-arena.com/debug/effects (and, through WP-880, `/coverage`)

## Goal

Schemes are the only card type with **no row anywhere** in the
effect-implementation index (`data/metadata/effect-implementation-index.json`).
Heroes, Villains, Henchmen, Master Strikes and Mastermind Tactics all have
rows; the 200 Schemes across 39 sets have none. So no coverage surface can say
whether Brainwash the Military's Twist or Evil Wins is implemented. This WP
adds a fourth, **`scheme`-scoped feed**. Each Scheme gets one `card × mechanic`
row per printed line kind (Setup, Special Rules, Twist, Evil Wins, Good Wins).
Its `status` comes from a curated overlay, seeded for the 12 Core and Captain
America 75th Schemes, which have live-verify evidence. Every other Scheme reads
`unmarked` until someone audits it.

## User-Visible Impact

On `/debug/effects`, a **Scheme** filter chip appears beside Hero / Villain /
Mastermind, and the summary line shows a scheme count. Filtering to Scheme lists
every Scheme's lines. The 12 seeded Schemes show `executable` wherever a line
is evidenced (AC-4); the rest show `unmarked`. WP-880 then counts these rows in the
`/coverage` set headline.

## Context (Read First)

**Why now.** Jeff asked on 2026-11-10 why `/coverage` read 100% for Captain
America 75th when its four Schemes were never counted. Two answers:
- **The interim fix:** #3256 relabelled the headline "hero cards only".
- **The real fix** is that every card type in a set feeds the headline (WP-880).
  WP-880 needs Scheme rows to exist first, and this WP adds them.

**Why one row per line kind, not one per Scheme.** A Scheme is not one effect.
Its Setup, Special Rules, Twist and Evil Wins are implemented by different engine
code: setup builders, `schemeTwistResolvers.ts`, the loss conditions in
`schemeTwistConfigs.ts`. They also go live separately. WP-763's audit found
Schemes with a printed loss but a `counter-only` Twist. A single row would hide
exactly that gap. Hero rows are already per card × mechanic, so this matches.

**Why a curated overlay, not a classifier.** The generator is deliberately
engine-free (it reads committed JSON plus card data). A Scheme's status cannot
be read from card data, and reading `schemeTwistConfigs.ts` would couple the
generator to engine source. So this follows WP-507 and WP-867: identity is
generated, provenance is curated.

**Why seed only Core and Captain America 75th.** Those 12 Schemes have
live-verify evidence on record. Marking other Schemes `executable` from
`schemeTwistConfigs.ts` alone is the reasoned-not-observed claim the honesty
rule forbids. A config entry can be `counter-only` (no Twist effect), and a
Setup line can be partly honored. The other 188 read `unmarked`, which is the
honest "no curated evidence" signal the tactic and strike feeds already use.
Auditing them set by set is a follow-up, not this WP.

**Determinism / boundaries.** This is a build-time coverage artifact plus its
schema and the dashboard reader:
- no engine, no `G`, no runtime;
- no `finalStateHash` or sentinel surface;
- the registry edit is a schema-only, additive enum widening (a contract change,
  so D-24828).

## Design Rationale

**Line-kind classification is a closed map on the printed label.** A printed
line's label is the text before its first `:`, reduced in two locked steps:

1. Strip a trailing quoted title: `/\s*“[^“”]*[“”]\s*$/`. This turns
   `Twist 7“Unleash Weapon X“` into `Twist 7`.
2. Strip a trailing Twist-number list:
   `/\s+\d+(?:\s*-\s*\d+)?(?:\s*,\s*(?:and\s+)?\d+)*$/`. This covers a
   single number, a range (`1-6`), a comma list (`1,3,5,7` / `1, 3, 5, 7, 9`)
   and an "and" list (`1, 3, and 5`).

The reduced label maps to the mechanic below:

| Printed label | `mechanic` |
|---|---|
| `Setup` | `setup` |
| `Special Rules`, `When revealed` | `special-rules` |
| `Twist`, `Twists`, `Other Twists`, `Master Strike or Twist` | `twist` |
| `Evil Wins`, `Chthon Wins` | `evil-wins` |
| `Good Wins`, `When the players win` | `good-wins` |

**Any other line folds into `special-rules`.** That covers unlabelled rules
text, `[hc:…]` lead-ins and the JSON-looking `{"points":…}` **strings** (the
data has no non-string abilities). This is deliberate, but it has one known
side effect: six Schemes gain a `special-rules` row only from the sub-bullets of
a Twist, or from reminder text. Those six are 2099 Subjugate Earth, bkpt Provoke
a Clash of Nations, rlmk War of Kings, shld Hail Hydra, mdns Wager at Blackjack,
and mdns Great Old One Chthon. The rule is kept for determinism (no "continuation
line" heuristic) and is recorded in D-24828 and the overlay `_comment`. All the Scheme's lines
are still accounted for, and the closed vocabulary does not grow per Scheme.
Each Scheme gets **one row per distinct mechanic it prints** (a Scheme with
three Twist lines has one `twist` row), across all of its cards. Under this
rule the feed emits **664** scheme rows: setup 195, special-rules 104, twist 200,
evil-wins 154, good-wins 11 (measured at the baseline).

**A loss printed inside a Twist line counts in the `twist` row.** 46 Schemes
have no `evil-wins` row, for three reasons:
- 31 print their loss on a Twist line (for example `Twist 8: Evil Wins!`);
- 10 print only a Good Wins condition;
- 5 print neither.

So a missing `evil-wins` row does **not** mean a missing loss condition.

**Ext_id grammar.** `${setAbbr}-scheme-${schemeSlug}` — disjoint from hero
(`{set}/{hero}`), villain (`{set}-villain-…`) and mastermind
(`{set}-mastermind-…`) ids, so the generator's cross-feed collision check
stays meaningful. `set` is the **file-level `abbr`**, as in WP-507.

## Contract

- **Scope enum:** `EFFECT_INDEX_ENTRY_SCOPES` widens to `["hero", "villain",
  "mastermind", "scheme"]`, and `EffectImplementationSummarySchema.byScope`
  gains a required `scheme: number`.
- **Scheme entries:** each published entry with `scope: "scheme"` has:
  - `extId`: `${setAbbr}-scheme-${schemeSlug}`

  - provenance: verbatim from the overlay, or `unmarked` + blanks
- **Tally:** `byScope.scheme` equals the scheme-entry count (the existing
  refinement enforces it).
- **`scripts/coverage/scheme-provenance.json`:** `{ "schemaVersion": 1,
  "_comment": "…", "schemes": { "<scheme-ext_id>": { "<mechanic>": { "status",
  "handler", "wp", "decision" } } } }`. The orphan check iterates `schemes` only:
  every key there must name an enumerated Scheme, and every mechanic under it
  must be one that Scheme prints. Otherwise the build fails, and it also fails on
  an `executable` row with a blank `handler`.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `EFFECT_INDEX_ENTRY_SCOPES` = `["hero", "villain", "mastermind", "scheme"]`.
   The schema accepts a `scheme` entry and `byScope.scheme`, rejects a wrong
   `byScope.scheme` count, and still rejects an unknown scope.
2. The generator emits one `scheme` entry per (Scheme, distinct printed
   mechanic) across all 39 sets, and `byScope.scheme` equals that count.
3. Classification follows the locked map:
   - `anni/pulse-waves-from-the-negative-zone`'s `Twist 1,3,5,7:` line is `twist`;
   - an unlabelled line (e.g. `dead/deadpool-wants-a-chimichanga`'s
     "Nobody Eats Just One Chimichanga!") is `special-rules`;
   - `core/unleash-the-power-of-the-cosmic-cube` has no `evil-wins` row: its loss
     is printed on `Twist 8:` and counts in the `twist` row;
   - `dstr/duels-of-science-and-magic` (`Twist 1, 3, and 5`) and
     `wpnx/condition-logan-into-weapon-x` (`Twist 7“Unleash Weapon X“`) are
     `twist`, and neither gets a spurious `special-rules` row.
4. Every row of the 12 seeded Schemes is either `executable` or named in the
   execution summary as unevidenced.
   - An `executable` row requires a `handler`.
   - `wp` is the WP that built the line and `decision` is its D-id. Each is
     copied when a source names it, and otherwise left `""`.
   - A blank `wp` or `decision` does not disqualify a row (for example,
     Negative Zone's `|twist` record has no wp).
   Example: `ca75-scheme-brainwash-the-military` has `setup`, `special-rules`,
   `twist` and `evil-wins` rows.
5. An unseeded Scheme (e.g. `anni-scheme-sneak-attack-the-heroes-homes`) reads
   `unmarked` with blank handler, wp and decision.
6. An overlay key or mechanic that matches no enumerated Scheme line, or an
   `executable` row with a blank handler, makes the generator throw. The error
   message names the key; tests match on the message, since `TransformFailure`
   is not exported.
7. `effect-index:check` and `effect-index:test` pass.
8. `/debug/effects` shows a **Scheme** chip, the scheme count and scheme rows
   with the `.fx-scope-scheme` badge.
9. Registry `dist` rebuilt; `pnpm -r build` 0; registry and dashboard suites
   green.
