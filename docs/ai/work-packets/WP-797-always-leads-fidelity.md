## Goal

A mastermind's printed **Always Leads** names either a Villain Group or a **Henchman Group** (rulebook v23 setup:
"'Always Leads' a particular Villain/Adversary Group or Henchman Group/Backup Adversaries. Be sure to include that
group", `docs/legendary-universal-rules-v23.md` :441–446). The card data can express only Villain-Group leads, and
several Villain-Group leads disagree with the printed card.

After this packet:
- the 9 masterminds that print a Henchman-Group lead carry it in a new `alwaysLeadsHenchmen` field, and the loadout
  builder auto-includes and locks that group;
- the 11 verified printed-vs-data Villain-Group mismatches are corrected at their source (`leads.json` /
  hand-authored `co2e.json`);
- the gauntlet menus anchor Henchman leads in the Henchman slots and never put a Henchman group in a Villain slot;
- core Dr. Doom's ranked gauntlet legs no longer treat Masters of Evil as his Always-Leads anchor.

## User-Visible Impact

- **Loadout builder (cards):** selecting core Dr. Doom auto-adds and locks `core/doombot-legion` in Henchmen, and no
  longer forces `core/masters-of-evil`. Magus, J. Jonah Jameson, Mandarin, Spider-Queen, Odin, Killmonger the
  Betrayer, Ultron Infinity and co2e Doctor Doom behave the same way with their printed Henchman group. Hela, Malekith,
  Thanos, Ebony Maw, Mephisto and Alchemax Executives require their printed Villain group. msp1 Loki, Kang Quantum
  Conqueror and co2e Doctor Octopus no longer force a second group. Omega Red and Ego force none ("Any Villain Group").
- **Card detail (cards):** a mastermind's raw JSON shows the corrected `alwaysLeads` / `alwaysLeadsHenchmen`. `ledBy`
  is not on FlatCards; it lives only in the per-set JSON (R2 `metadata/`).
- **Ranked gauntlet (play):** core Dr. Doom's 1–4-player legs field Brotherhood / Enemies of Asgard / HYDRA (Skrulls
  on the four "Skrulls replace Brotherhood" legs) instead of Masters of Evil first. The 16 masterminds whose menu
  changes (§Locked Contract Values) launch with the corrected composition. co2e Doctor Doom's menu no longer lists
  `co2e/doombot-legion` as a Villain group.
- **Themes:** "Annihilation Conquest" (Magus) and "House of M" (Mandarin) prefill their mastermind's printed Henchman
  group instead of showing a missing-required warning.

## Context (Read First)

**How it was found.** The WP-796 live-verify (2026-10-07) showed the builder locking Masters of Evil for core Dr. Doom,
whose card reads "Always Leads: Doombot Legion". A scan of all 111 masterminds' printed "Always Leads:" text against
`alwaysLeads`, with every disputed case checked against the card image on R2, found:

| Kind | Mastermind (set) | Printed | Data today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Henchman lead, wrong villain anchor | core Dr. Doom | Doombot Legion | `["masters-of-evil"]` |
| Henchman slug in `alwaysLeads` | co2e Doctor Doom | Doombot Legions | `["doombot-legion"]` (+ `masters-of-evil.ledBy` = doctor-doom) |
| Henchman lead dropped | cosm Magus · dims J. Jonah Jameson · rvlt Mandarin · ssw2 Spider-Queen · vill Odin · wtif Killmonger the Betrayer · wtif Ultron Infinity | their Henchman group | `[]` |
| Swapped | asrd Hela ↔ Malekith | Omens of Ragnarok / Dark Council | reversed |
| Swapped | msis Thanos ↔ Ebony Maw | Infinity Stones / Children of Thanos | reversed |
| Wrong group | dkcy Mephisto | Underworld | `emissaries-of-evil` |
| Wrong group | 2099 Alchemax Executives | Alchemax Enforcers | `false-aesir-of-alchemax` |
| Over-requires | msp1 Loki | Enemies of Asgard | + `chitauri` |
| Over-requires | amwp Kang, Quantum Conqueror | Armada of Kang (an extra group is set aside, not led) | + `quantum-realm` |
| Over-requires | co2e Doctor Octopus | Any "Sinister" Villain Group | both Sinister groups |
| Forced specific for "Any" | wpnx Omega Red · mgtg Ego | Any Villain Group | `berserkers` / `ravagers` |

`co2e/doctor-doom`'s henchman slug in `alwaysLeads` already breaks a shipped artifact: its generated menu puts
`co2e/doombot-legion` in **both** `villainGroupIds` and `henchmanGroupIds` (`gauntletLoadouts.generated.ts` :323–327).

**Decisions taken with Jeff (2026-10-07):**
1. **A new field, not a mixed one.** `alwaysLeads` stays Villain groups only; `alwaysLeadsHenchmen` carries Henchman
   leads; Henchman groups gain a symmetric `ledBy`. A mixed array is what let co2e Doom's henchman slug reach a villain
   slot.
2. **One WP** for the Henchman support and the 11 Villain-lead corrections: same files, one regeneration.
3. **Re-anchor Dr. Doom's ranked legs** (not metadata-only): his authored villain pools drop Masters of Evil from the
   anchor slot, accepting the D-24666-style ranked consequence.

**The re-anchor is not invented.** With `alwaysLeads` empty, the generator's own D-24199 fill gives Dr. Doom
`core/brotherhood`, `core/enemies-of-asgard`, `core/hydra`, `core/masters-of-evil` in fill order. The authored
null-`variety` legs must equal the menu (`gauntletConfigs.test.ts` :114–165), so their pool becomes exactly that order;
the four "Skrulls replace Brotherhood" legs apply their swap to it. Masters of Evil stays in every pool at 5 players.

**Any-of leads (locked rule, D-24667 §2).** "Any Villain Group" → no forced lead (`alwaysLeads: []`, `leads.json`
marks `"_anyVillainGroup": true`, the wtif Hank Pym precedent). "Any X or Y Villain Group" → exactly one in-set
qualifying group, keeping today's pick when it qualifies (2099 Sinister Six → `alchemax-enforcers`; co2e Magneto →
`brotherhood-of-mutants`; co2e Doctor Octopus → `sinister-spider-foes`). Data never requires more groups than printed.

## D-24667 Content (authored at execution, Status Active)

1. **Data shape.** `alwaysLeads` is Villain groups only; `alwaysLeadsHenchmen` carries printed Henchman-group leads;
   Henchman groups carry a symmetric `ledBy`. Extends D-16703. The rulebook allows either kind (v23 setup).
2. **Printed text wins.** Leads match the printed card. "Any Villain Group" → no forced lead; "Any X or Y" → one in-set
   qualifying group (keep the existing pick when it qualifies). Data never requires more groups than printed.
3. **Pipeline.** Both converters honour `leads.json` `henchmen`; outlier lead arrays are rebuilt from `leads.json`,
   never appended; the generator rejects a lead slug of the wrong type.
4. **Gauntlet and ranked.** Henchman leads anchor Henchman slots. Dr. Doom's config anchor becomes
   `anchorHenchmanGroup: core/doombot-legion` and his villain pools take the generator's fill order (Masters of Evil
   last). His 1–4-player compositions change, so 24 `scenario_key`s change: seed PAR v1 is regenerated in place (the
   WP-599 precedent), replacing exactly those 24 artifacts. Pre-change Dr. Doom score rows stay in the database but
   stop counting toward gauntlet standings and run progress; scenario boards keep them under their old keys. The menu
   compositions of 16 masterminds change, so their menu-fallback approved loadouts move the same way. **Accepted by
   decision** (only the operator has scores); row cleanup is a named follow-up. The config anchor is exactly one of
   `anchorVillainGroup` / `anchorHenchmanGroup`: a mastermind that prints both a Villain-Group and a Henchman-Group lead
   (which `leads.json` allows) cannot be authored in `gauntlet-configs.json` until the config schema is revised.
5. **Builder.** The loadout builder auto-includes, locks and requires the led Henchman group, as it does villains,
   and the Cards-tab toggle cannot remove it (the D-24054 guard extended to Henchmen).
6. **Not covered:** solo-mode exemption, Kang / Ego setup extras, a true one-of constraint, Dr. Doom PAR profiles,
   the content-toolkit schema, ssw1 unassigned groups. Named follow-ups.
