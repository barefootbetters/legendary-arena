## Goal

As a player who defeats one of the Mandarin's Rings henchmen (Revelations), I want its printed Fight effect to happen. After WP-870, three of the ten variants still do nothing and log an `unmarked-ability` hollow in Play Diagnostics. After this session they do what they print:

- **Liar, The Mento-Intensifier** — "Look at the top card of another player's deck. Say it is "Good" or "Bad." That player chooses to put it in your discard pile or their discard pile."
- **Nightbringer, The Black Light** — "Reveal the top three cards of the Villain Deck. You may defeat a Villain you revealed worth 2VP or less. (Do its Fight effect.) Put the rest back in any order."
- **Remaker, The Matter Rearranger** — "You may choose a card from your hand or discard pile. The player on your right puts it in their hand."

## User-Visible Impact

On play.legendary-arena.com:

- Defeating **Liar** with two or more seats lets you pick the other player (no prompt when there is only one). You privately see the top card of that player's deck and choose to say "Good" or "Bad" (you may lie). The log shows your claim, never the card. That player then chooses, without seeing the card, whether it goes in your discard pile or theirs, and the log names the card once it lands. In a one-seat game nothing happens (logged).
- Defeating **Nightbringer** reveals the top three Villain Deck cards in the log. When any of them is a Villain or Henchman worth 2VP or less, you may defeat one (it goes to your Victory Pile). Then you choose the order of the cards that are left (two cards: two orders; three: six). Then the defeated Villain's Fight effect happens, so a Fight effect that plays Villain Deck cards plays from the order you just set. A Fight effect that needs the City space it was fought in does nothing (there is none).
- Defeating **Remaker** with two or more seats lets you give a card from your hand or discard pile (or nothing) to the player on your right, who puts it in their hand. In a one-seat game nothing happens (logged).
- These cards no longer appear as hollow effects in Play Diagnostics.

The prompts use the seat-choice panel's generic "Your choice" heading. The option labels carry the context (§Out of Scope: card-specific headings are a client follow-up).

## Rulings (Jeff, 2026-11-07, before locking)

The v23 universal rules (`docs/legendary-universal-rules-v23.md`) do not address any of the three cards. The Mandarin's Rings entry (L3088-3093) gives no per-card rulings. Jeff ruled on each recommendation below in the drafting session:

1. **Liar's statement is modelled.** Only the fighter sees the card, and they pick "Good" or "Bad" and may lie (the card is named Liar). The claim is logged for everyone. The other player picks the discard pile without seeing the card. The client has no in-game chat, so this prompt is the only way the target hears the claim. The card's name enters the log only once it is in a discard pile, which is public. Bot and timeout defaults: the fighter says "Good", and the target puts the card in the fighter's discard pile.
2. **Solo: Liar and Remaker do nothing.** Rules L3257-3261 apply "do it yourself" only to a Villain's "**each other player**" text, and neither card says that. Cards that mean you in solo print a note: amwp "(in solo mode, say one yourself)", bkpt "(In solo, you do both.)". Advanced Solo turns Betrayal's "the player to your right gains" into a KO, not a self-gain (L2712-2716, L3278-3279). D-24500 makes Random Acts' pass-left a solo no-op. "Solo" means one seat in `G.playerZones`. A human with a bot ally has two seats and plays the multiplayer path.
3. **Nightbringer: put back, then the Fight effect.** The chosen Villain goes to the Victory Pile when picked. You then order the remaining revealed cards on top of the Villain Deck. Then its Fight effect resolves. Fight effects occur after the card is in the Victory Pile (L5164-5167; Astral Plane's "put it in your Victory Pile and do its Fight effect", L1034-1036). Henchmen are Villains (L2923). VP is the printed VP that the WP-865 Heist reader returns. Defeating this way is a defeat, not a fight: no fight cost, no "whenever you fight" offers (Baron Zemo's paid rescue, the Enigma peek, Excessive Violence), the Silent Sniper free-defeat precedent. A Fight effect gated on the fought City space fails closed (D-24295). The cost of this ruling: a Fight effect that touches the Villain Deck (co2e Endless Armies of HYDRA "play two cards from the Villain Deck", cvwr Mr. Immortal, xmen Sapien League) sees the reordered cards instead of the fourth card. The fighter chose that order knowing what the Fight effect does. Holding the cards aside instead would need a hashed hold field that survives across moves.

"The player on your right" is the previous seat in turn order. Turns go clockwise (L524), so the next player is on your left, as D-24500 / WP-683 already encode. Clockwise is ascending seat id (D-24780, `turn/seatOrder.logic.ts`), so the right neighbour is the last seat of `seatsInTurnOrderFrom(seatIds, fighter)`.

## Context (Read First)

- **Why a separate WP from WP-870.** WP-870 reuses three shipped queues for the own-deck lines. These three each need a new cross-player or Villain Deck mechanic and a ruling (§Rulings). They share one shape: a pick the villain executor cannot park, opened from `onMove`, chained across one to three seat choices. One queue, one opener and one module keep the lockstep cost to one `G` field instead of three.
- **One queue, keyed by primitive.** `G.pendingVillainFightPicks` entries carry the triggering primitive, so no new union of pick kinds is needed. The opener shifts an entry off the front when it parks that entry's first seat choice. From then on every step's state lives in the choice's `villainFightPick` payload, so a chain never re-reads the queue.
- **Effect in the apply, next park in the chain.** Each kind's apply (`applySeatChoiceByKind`) does the step's own state change, ctx-free, so a defaulted choice (the timeout path) still moves the card. Two things run only in the `resolveSeatChoice` chain section, because they need the move's `events`, `ctx` or `random`: parking the next step (a non-active seat needs `events.setActivePlayers`), and Nightbringer's Fight effect (the executor's trace turn and the bridges' `RevealContext`). The timeout path does not chain. That is the D-24500 / D-24501 residual every chained kind shares, since the path is not wired live.
- **Nightbringer and the bridges.** Defeating from the Villain Deck must run the same Fight fire-site bridges as a City defeat. Otherwise Endless Armies of HYDRA (2VP) would play nothing and Mr. Immortal would not reenter. The bridges are extracted verbatim from `defeatCityVillainCore` into one helper that both the core and the new deck-defeat path call. Any later bridge (WP-869's Groot bridge) then reaches both. The core's behavior is byte-identical.
- **Nightbringer and converted cards.** A Skrull, Brainwash Officer or Micro-Sized Hero in the Villain Deck is a Villain worth 1 VP, so Nightbringer may defeat it, and a defeat routes it the way any defeat does. The core's origin routing therefore moves verbatim into one helper (`routeDefeatedVillainCard`) that the City core and the Villain Deck defeat both call. A Micro-Sized card defeated this way goes to the defeater. "When you fight one, choose any player" is a fight trigger, and the Nightbringer step's own seat choice is still open during the apply, so `gainDefeatedMicroSizedHero` takes its logged fallback (the D-24805 residual, recorded in D-24817).
- **Nightbringer and requirements.** Only `fightVillain` checks "can't defeat X unless …" requirements and the WP-871 fight restrictions. A free defeat ignores them (D-24737, the Silent Sniper / Cruel Ruler precedent), and so does Nightbringer. D-24817 records this as a decision.
- **A Ring can defeat a Ring.** Rings are 1 VP, so Nightbringer may defeat Liar or Remaker from the deck. Their Fight effect queues a pick during the resolve chain, and the post-move opener opens it (T14b).
- **Hidden information.** The only places the looked-at card appears before it lands are the fighter's `liar-claim` option labels (their own prompt) and the unprojected payload. The target's `liar-decide` labels carry the claim, never the card. A filter test pins both (T9).
- **Remaker's card is public.** The log names the given card, as Random Acts of Unkindness names a passed hand card (D-24500). The hand-to-hand move goes through `moveCardFromZone`, which is not a gain, so no "gain" reactions fire. A Wound from your discard pile is a legal choice; the card does not restrict it.
- **Determinism.** RNG only through the existing reshuffle (`ShuffleProvider` in the handler, `random` in the move). One new lazy `G` field, dropped at game end and turn start. The opener runs identically in the live `onMove` and the rebuilt loops. `replay/replay.execute.ts` is deliberately not given the opener (the D-24322 onMove-parity exclusion); server replay uses the boardgame.io reducer, which runs `onMove`. Hash oracles do not move (no sentinel uses Mandarin's Rings).
