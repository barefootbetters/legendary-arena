## Closing Notes

The single fail-open boundary is the load-bearing invariant: the entire
"a marketing outage must never break signup" guarantee lives in
`enqueuePlayerToMarketingList`'s `try/catch`, so every caller (today the
resolver; tomorrow any other provisioning path) inherits it for free without
re-implementing the guard. The factory + back-compat-const shape is the other
piece worth flagging — it lets a locked 2-arg interface (`AccountResolver`)
gain an optional dependency without widening the contract or introducing a
module-level singleton, a pattern future server hooks can reuse.
