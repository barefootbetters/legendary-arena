### 9. Forward-Safety Questions

- **WP-741 (repeated gates).** Order-independent: the hook keeps whatever conditions the parser
  builds, so WP-741's collapse applies to Sniper Squad and Reshape Reality automatically.
- **Enemy-sent Out of Time (anni).** The storage and guard 1 already admit it: a villain primitive
  that sets a card aside must append to `outOfTimeCards` and the replay will play it once; recording
  it in the ledger at replay blocks a re-send.
- **Winter Soldier 2>4.** Needs a hand-card chooser and a per-turn granted-keyword map that the
  handler consults; its lines stay honest hollows (`man-out-of-time` 13 runtime observations).
- **End-of-game scoring.** "All your cards" reads (Ultron) do not see a set-aside card if the game
  ends between its two turns; named follow-up.
- **Pre-deploy logs** that played a recognized line no longer re-execute byte-identically (the
  D-24687 §4 window).
