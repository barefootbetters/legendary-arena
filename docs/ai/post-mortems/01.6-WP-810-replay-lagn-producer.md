### 9. Forward-Safety Questions

- [x] Survives refactors without touching other layers: yes — server-only logic, one new pure module, the engine used only through its read-only surface.
- [x] Replay reconstructs behavior from stored data: yes, by construction — the whole feature IS the re-execution, verified against a fresh reduction on the serving build. No stored record migrates (the writer flip is a pure restamp; readers already accept every version back to 1.0.0).
