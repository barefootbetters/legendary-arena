### 6. Hidden-Coupling Detection

- [x] **Overflow flattens 3D.** A non-`visible` overflow forces its subtree flat and clips it, so
  the straighten exists only while `.city-spaces` / `.hq-slots` are `overflow: visible`, which
  is only safe while the row fits. Any future `overflow`, `opacity < 1` or `filter` on an element
  between the plane and a board button breaks the lift silently in jsdom; the guard's
  `isHoverChainIntact` walk is the only check that sees it.
- [x] **Row-fit coupling.** The lift reads the slash gesture's own `measureRowFits` (now exported).
  A change to that fit logic moves both features together, which is the intent: both depend on
  "this row does not scroll sideways".
- [x] **Chooser depth order.** Inside a 3D context depth order beats `z-index`; the
  `:not(.city-space--choosing)` exclusion keeps a space with an open WP-817 chooser from lifting.
  Any future overlay inside a City space or HQ slot needs the same treatment.
- [x] **Stroke-start measurement.** `collectCandidateTiles` (`useSlashGesture.ts`) measures each
  tile once at stroke start, when the card under the pointer may be lifted, and pointer capture
  on the row can then drop the hover mid-stroke while the stroke keeps the lifted rect. The
  guard pins that neighbouring City boxes keep a gap with a card lifted
  (`hasCityBoxGapsWhileHovered`: 8.6 px at 1280×720, the tightest width); the D-24026 live slash
  check covers the behaviour end to end.
- [x] **3D hit-testing (found at execution, EC Amendment A).** The locked design kept the
  `preserve-3d` chain on at rest. Chrome z-fights coplanar 3D layers in its hit test, and the
  plane took up to 60% of a resting HQ card's clicks at 1280×720. Every geometry invariant and
  the prototype screenshots still looked right; only hit-testing showed it. The chain now
  switches on through `:has(…:hover)` only while a liftable card is hovered, and the plane steps
  out of hit testing while it is 3D. The guard gained `isRestingCardHitIntact`. Lesson for any
  future 3D board work: a `preserve-3d` context needs a hit-test check, not just a geometry one.
- [x] **Hover-out.** When the pointer leaves, the `:has` stops matching at once, so the card
  settles back flattened into the plane during its 150 ms transition instead of in 3D. At this
  angle the in-between frames differ by a few percent of height; accepted, noted for the
  live-verify.
