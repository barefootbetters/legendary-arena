## 4. Weaknesses & Conflicts

- **Physical / power-level weaknesses:** Purely peak-human (no flight, invulnerability, or healing factor); vulnerable to superhuman foes and heavy damage; dependent on gear and environment.
- **Internal / psychological conflicts:** Deep guilt over Red Room assassinations and brainwashing; infertility from the serum; Red Room trigger-word conditioning and implanted false memories that resurface under stress; inability to fully trust or be trusted.
- **Signature dilemmas:** "The darker parts of her ledger" — can a lifelong traitor/spy ever truly become a hero, or will her past always demand atonement?

## 5. Key Story Arcs (max 3)

1. **Defection from KGB (1964–1970s)** — From Soviet assassin to Avengers ally; redemption through betrayal of her handlers.
2. **Widow's Hunt (2009–2010)** — Vengeance against Red Room remnants; loss and rage while confronting her mentor's fate.
3. **Secret Empire (2017)** — Moral despair and sacrifice; trains new heroes while facing the ultimate test of loyalty vs. corruption.
